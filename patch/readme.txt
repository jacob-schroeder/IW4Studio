This default_mp.self includes native gsc parsing, compilation and linking for TU 1.14.
This utilizes a patched executable and connects online while enabling GSC.
Additionally this patch resolves a bug where PSN accounts created after or changed after 2018 were not syncing stats with the Activision DemonWare server.

TO USE:
Simply replace default_mp.self in your game update directory (usdir) with this supplied executable.

Enjoy!
